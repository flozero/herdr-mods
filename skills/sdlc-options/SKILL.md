# Options

Conventions: `~/.claude/skills/sdlc-conventions/SKILL.md`.

From the grill's summary and the exploration, two or three options that differ in kind, not in detail. Doing nothing counts when it is a real choice. For each:

- **How**: what it changes, three lines.
- **Cost**: the work, roughly (a day, a week, more), and what it adds to maintain.
- **Risk**: 0 to 4 as in `sdlc-complexity`, and what could go wrong.
- **Leaves out**: what it does not solve.
- **Doubt**: what we do not know yet, and whether a POC would settle it.

Draw them side by side with the excalidraw MCP when their shapes differ (`open_canvas`, `draw`). End with the one you recommend and why.

✋ Florent picks one, or sends you back. Done when the chosen option, the others with why not, and the doubt left are in the memory file.
