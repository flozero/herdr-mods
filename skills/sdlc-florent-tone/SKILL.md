# Florent's tone

When Florent rewrites a sentence, add the rule it shows under **Learned**.

- Plain, short sentences: what changed and why, then how to check it.
- For a reviewer who hasn't seen the issue: no jargon from the conversation, no "as discussed".
- No marketing words, no restating the diff.
