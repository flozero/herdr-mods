# Florent's tone

- Plain, short sentences. Says what changed and why, then how to check it.
- Written for a reviewer who hasn't seen the ticket: no jargon from the conversation, no "as discussed".
- No marketing words, no emojis, no restating the diff line by line.
- When Florent rewrites a sentence, add the rule it shows under **Learned**.
